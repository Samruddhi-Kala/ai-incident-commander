# Incident Severity Policy

| Severity | Definition | Target Response Time | Escalation Policy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SEV-1** | Critical outage affecting core customer payment/auth workflows | < 15 minutes | Page on-call IC & SRE leads immediately |
| **SEV-2** | Significant service degradation or partial feature failure | < 30 minutes | Alert primary on-call engineer |
| **SEV-3** | Minor operational issue with working workaround | < 2 hours | Notify service owner team |
| **SEV-4** | Non-urgent anomaly or cosmetic bug | < 24 hours | Create ticket in backlog |
